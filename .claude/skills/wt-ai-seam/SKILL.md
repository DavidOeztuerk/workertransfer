# The AI seam (ADR-0024, ADR-0051)

binding one-liner stays in `CLAUDE.md`, „The rules that carry the design".

### The AI seam drafts on request and stores nothing (ADR-0024)

There are **two** consumers and they are mirror images. `POST /profiles/me/draft` helps a *person* say what they want to say; `POST /jobs/draft` helps a *company* phrase **its own advert**. Neither ever says anything *about* anyone — that is what makes the second one buildable without its own weighing, where a scout, a candidate ranking or a salary recommendation would all aim at people.

Each service owns its own `IDrafter` port and its own context type rather than sharing one prompt with branches — the profile context carries no name, no email, no subject id, no employer, no CV; the job context carries no tenant and no company name. Tests pin both field sets. A shared prompt with an `if` is exactly where "invent nothing about the person" would one day apply to an advert, or worse, the reverse.

**Nothing writes by itself, and a cover letter least of all** (21.10.2026, `docs/GESCHAEFTSMODELL-FRAGEN.md`). A writing costs the person's AI credit, so an application draft begins in state **`open`** and waits for her click — "Mit KI schreiben" or "Selbst schreiben"; the bulk apply writes only with "Anschreiben von der KI vorschreiben lassen" ticked. It used to begin at `generating`, and `StartIfGenerating` requeues such a draft on every GET: every look at an application would have spent credit. Every uploaded document goes along by default and is deselected per application.

**One AI access rule, and the platform's model is the local Ollama until the start** ([ADR-0051](docs/adr/0051-ein-ki-zugang-plattform-modell-und-eigener-schluessel.md), 17.10.2026). Every seam on the person's side — cover letter, profile help, reply drafts, the advisor — takes **her own key if she stored one, otherwise the platform's model**; a silent identity-service is refused, never quietly replaced. Own keys are **Anthropic or OpenAI** for now, and the **address is set by the platform** (`AiProviders` in identity's domain), never typed: the egress boundary allows exactly `api.anthropic.com` and `api.openai.com` as `Draft__AllowedTargets__*`. Without a key identity hands out "no own access" whatever provider is chosen. **The company's seams (advert help, scout approach, company replies) take the company's own key since 21.10.2026** (ADR-0051 Ergänzung, Bauliste 6): an `admin` stores it at `/company/settings`, identity keeps it encrypted in `company_ai_settings`, and `ServiceDefaults/Ai/CompanyAiAccess.cs` asks `GET /internal/companies/{id}/ai` per draft, no cache. Same rule as the person's: own key, else the platform's model, a silent identity refused. **A 404 from that door is a refused secret, never "no key"** — read as "none" it would send every company's drafts to the platform's provider. The company selects the access and never enters the prompt. A member's private key never pays for the company. **A company's own OpenAI-compatible server** (`own`) is the one provider with a typed address, so it is bound: https, a host name (no IP literal) on the company's proven domain or below it, checked by identity against the company and never the request. Every company access then goes through the `company-model` client, which connects to public addresses only (`ServiceDefaults/PublicAddress`, shared with ADR-0052's feeds) and follows no redirect; the egress boundary learns the host from identity's answer just before the call (`CompanyAiEgress`, wrapping jobs' `CompanyDomainEgress`). The platform's model keeps its own client — the local Ollama lies inside on purpose. All four seams speak through `ServiceDefaults/Ai/ModelWire.cs` (`openai_compatible` default, `anthropic`); compose fills `Draft__*` from `WORKERTRANSFER_AI_{KEY,PROVIDER,URL,MODEL}`, defaulting to `http://host.docker.internal:11434/v1/chat/completions`. **The key is the switch**: empty means no platform model (CI, E2E); Ollama reads none, so any value switches it on. **Anthropic thinks before it writes**: `ModelWire.Text` reads the first *text* block, Anthropic requests carry at least 16 000 `max_tokens` and effort `low`, a refusal is no draft. `make ai-check` proves the model answers the real way; `make e2e` and `make route-map` switch it off for their run, as CI (`scripts/without-model.sh`) — and since 21.10.2026 the Google/Microsoft sign-in too, whose real client ids in a developer's `.env` turn the map's `404` on `/auth/external/google/*` into `200`. Which provider the platform runs in operation — and the subscription-or-quota idea — is decided with the business model before the start.

**Known values leave as placeholders** (`ServiceDefaults/Ai/Placeholders.cs`, VISION-FRAGEN E2): the cover-letter writer masks the person's name (and its parts), the addressee and every former employer in the *whole* instruction — they also stand in free text — and restores them in the answer and in every streamed fragment. It masks at the last step before the wire so no caller can forget it. It cannot find what a person typed about somebody else in free text; there the choice of provider protects.

With no API key configured, nothing external is ever called and the UI says so. Nothing is stored — not the prompt, not the answer; the draft lives in the form until it is saved, and then it is the author's text. Errors report the failure kind, never the content. Do not add memory, a vector store, or a background suggestion — all three either store or act unasked. **There is deliberately no plan-act-reflect loop and no ledger entry**: a reflect stage means two calls to rewrite the user's own words unasked, and a ledger entry would assert a standing permission nobody gave — the button is the consent, informed and per use.
